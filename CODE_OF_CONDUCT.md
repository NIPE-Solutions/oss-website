# Code of Conduct

NIPE Open Source is committed to a respectful, inclusive collaboration space.
Be constructive, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and changes
rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks
are not welcome.

Report concerns privately to
[office@nipesolutions.com](mailto:office@nipesolutions.com). Maintainers will
review reports discreetly and may remove content, limit participation, or take
other proportionate action to protect contributors and users.
